Open **Cash** under **Assets** on the home screen to view your dollar balance and earnings. The Cash screen separates funds that are idle from funds that are earning yield.

<img src="/Start%20Earning/cash-overview.png" alt="Cash screen showing Idle balance, Earn balance, today's earnings, lifetime earnings, and the weekly earnings chart" width="40%" />

## Your Cash Balance

- **Balance** — your total Cash balance.
- **Idle balance** — Cash that is not currently earning yield.
- **Earn balance** — Cash allocated to earning yield.
- **Today's earnings** — the amount earned today.
- **Lifetime earnings** — your total earnings to date.

The displayed APY is variable. Check the rate in the app when you start earning; the rate shown in the screenshot is an example.

## Start Earning

1. On the Cash screen, tap **Start earning** in the idle-balance banner, or tap **Earn/Pause** and select **Earn**.
2. Enter the amount you want to move into your Earn balance. Tap **Use maximum** to use the available balance shown on the screen.
3. Tap **Continue**.
4. On **Review transaction**, check the amount moving from **Idle Balance** to **Earn Balance**, along with the APY, fees, and processing time.
5. **Swipe up to earn** to confirm.
6. When **Earnings initiated!** appears, tap anywhere to continue. The confirmation says your amount will start earning shortly.

Return to Cash to see your updated Idle balance and Earn balance. The **Earn/Pause** control also provides a **Pause** option.

## Track Your Earnings

The **Earnings** card shows the amount earned **This week** and a daily bar chart. Tap **All time** to open the detailed **Earnings** screen.

Under **Axal Prime**, you can review:

- **Lifetime earnings** — your total earnings for the strategy.
- **Earning rate** — the current rate.
- **Asset** — USDC.
- **Earnings balance** — the balance allocated to earning.
- **Projected earnings** — the displayed yearly estimate, which can change with your balance and earning rate.

Scroll down to the **Earnings** list to see daily earnings by date. **Earn** and **Pause** controls are also available at the bottom of this screen.

## View Your Investments

On the detailed Earnings screen, **Investments** shows where the strategy distributes your assets. The allocation bar and investment list show the underlying markets, their allocation percentages, and rates. Tap an investment to open its protocol page for more details.

## Learn About Cash

Scroll down on the Cash screen to **Description** and tap **Read more** to expand it. Cash is held in USDC, and the app includes a **Website** link and answers to common questions:

- **What is USDC and is it safe?**
- **Why is the rate higher than my bank?**
- **Can the rate change?**

Use **Add funds** or **Withdraw** at the bottom of the Cash screen to access those flows.

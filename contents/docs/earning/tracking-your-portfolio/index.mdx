The Overview tab shows your combined portfolio at a glance. Here you can monitor your total balance and track how much you are earning over time.

## Reading the Chart

The solid line represents your **total portfolio balance** — the cumulative value of all your assets including principal and yield earned. The bars show **what you earned each day**. As you earn more, both the bars and the line will trend upward.

You can view your earnings across five time periods: **7D**, **30D**, **90D**, **1Y**, and **5Y**.

## Assets

Below the chart you will see your assets broken out by account:

- **USD Balance** — your idle USD not currently earning yield
- **USD Yield account** — your USD actively earning yield, shown with its current APY
- **BTC Balance** — your idle BTC not currently earning yield
- **BTC Yield account** — your BTC actively earning yield, shown with its current APY

Tap into the **USD** or **Bitcoin** tabs for a detailed breakdown of each asset.

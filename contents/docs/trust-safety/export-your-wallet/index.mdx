Because Axal is non-custodial, you can export your wallet's private key or recovery phrase at any
time and move your funds to a self-custody wallet such as MetaMask (EVM) or Phantom (Solana).

1. Go to **Profile → Security → Export wallet**.

<img src="/7702/Screen%20Shot%202025-09-10%20at%205.34.21%20PM.png" alt="Two-factor authentication modal" width="480" />

2. Enter your MFA code — see [Face ID, passkeys & 2FA](/docs/trust-safety/authenticator).

<img src="/7702/Screen%20Shot%202025-09-10%20at%205.13.37%20PM.png" alt="Export private key dropdown" width="480" />

3. Follow the prompts to reveal your recovery phrase or private key, then import it into your
   wallet of choice.

## What you'll need

- **Seed phrase (recovery phrase) or private key** — store it securely and never share it with
  anyone, including anyone claiming to be Axal support.
- **MFA code** — required to complete the export.

## Moving funds after exporting

Once imported into your own wallet:

- Select the correct network (Base, Ethereum, Solana, etc.)
- Paste the destination address and confirm the network fee
- Submit and wait for confirmation

## Notes

- Double-check addresses and networks before sending — transactions can't be reversed.
- Keep a small amount of the network's native gas token on hand to cover fees.
- Never store your seed phrase or private key in plaintext, in a note app, or in a screenshot.

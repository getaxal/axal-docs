This page explains how to move funds out of Axal. You can withdraw to a linked bank account, a finance app such as Coinbase or Robinhood, or an external crypto wallet. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required to complete any withdrawal.

## Withdraw to Bank Account

Bank withdrawals use your available **Cash balance** and require a six-digit code from your authenticator app.

### Choose Your Bank

1. Tap **Withdraw** on the Home screen.
2. In **Select asset**, choose **Cash**.
3. In **Choose withdrawal method**, select **Bank account**.
4. On **Bank Accounts**, select the bank account where you want to receive the withdrawal. If you need to link another bank, tap **Add an account**.
5. Tap **Continue**.

<img src="/Withdrawal/bank-withdrawal-method.png" alt="Choose withdrawal method screen with the Bank account option" width="40%" />

### Enter an Amount and Review

1. On **Withdraw Cash**, enter the amount you want to withdraw, or tap **Use maximum** to use the available balance shown.
2. Check the selected bank account at the bottom of the screen. Use the dropdown if you need to change it.
3. Tap **Continue**.
4. On **Review transaction**, check the withdrawal amount, **Cash Balance** funding source, destination bank account, **Fees**, and **Processing time**.

Axal charges no withdrawal fee. Bank withdrawals typically reach your account in **1–3 business days**.

### Confirm with Your Authenticator

1. Tap **Validate with TOTP** on the review screen.
2. Open your authenticator app and find the code for your Axal account.
3. Return to Axal and enter the six-digit code in **Enter your code**, or tap **Paste** if you copied it. If your phone asks for permission to paste, allow it to insert the code.
4. After successful verification, Axal confirms that the amount **is on its way to your bank account**.
5. Tap **Done** to return to the app.

<img src="/Withdrawal/bank-withdrawal-confirmation.png" alt="Confirmation that a withdrawal is on its way to the bank account, with a Done button" width="40%" />

The confirmation means your withdrawal has been submitted; bank processing still needs to complete.

## Withdraw to a Finance App

You can send funds from your available **Cash balance** to **Coinbase** or **Robinhood**. The steps below cover the Axal flow; sign-in and confirmation screens in the receiving app may differ.

### Choose an App

1. Tap **Withdraw** on the Home screen.
2. In **Select asset**, choose **Cash**.
3. In **Choose withdrawal method**, tap **Finance apps**.
4. In **Select app**, choose **Coinbase** or **Robinhood**.

<img src="/Withdrawal/finance-withdrawal-apps.png" alt="Select app screen with Coinbase and Robinhood withdrawal destinations" width="40%" />

### Enter an Amount and Review

1. On **Send Cash**, enter the amount you want to send, or tap **Use maximum** to use the available balance shown.
2. Check the receiving app at the bottom of the screen. Use the dropdown if you need to change it.
3. Tap **Continue**.
4. On **Review transaction**, check the amount, **Cash Balance** funding source, receiving app, **Fees**, and **Processing time**.

Axal charges no withdrawal fee. Check the processing estimate shown for your transaction. In the Robinhood example below, **Fees** shows **None** and **Processing time** shows **Instant**.

<img src="/Withdrawal/finance-withdrawal-review.png" alt="Review transaction screen for sending Cash to Robinhood, showing no fees and instant processing" width="40%" />

### Verify and Complete in the Receiving App

1. Tap **Validate with TOTP**.
2. Open your authenticator app and find the code for your Axal account.
3. Return to Axal and enter the six-digit code, or tap **Paste** if you copied it. Allow pasting if your phone asks.
4. After verification, continue to the selected app or its website. Sign in and follow its prompts to complete the transfer.
5. Check the receiving app for confirmation that the transfer has completed.

For Robinhood, stop screen recording or screen sharing if its security screen asks you to do so, then continue in Robinhood.

## Troubleshooting

- Withdrawal not showing? Bank withdrawals typically arrive in 1–3 business days. If it has been longer, contact support.
- Wrong network? Contact support at hello@getaxal.com with the transaction hash so the team can advise on recovery options.
- Note: Axal sponsors all gas costs for transactions within the Axal app, not external wallet transactions.

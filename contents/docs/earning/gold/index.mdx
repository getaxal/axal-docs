Open **Gold** under **Assets** on the home screen to view its price and your holdings. Gold in Axal is held as **Paxos Gold (PAXG)**. Gold buying is available, while **gold yield is coming soon**.

## Price and Balance

The chart at the top shows Gold's price and its change over the selected period. Choose **1D**, **7D**, **30D**, **90D**, or **All time** to change the period.

Below the chart, the balance card shows:

- **Balance** — the dollar value of your Gold holdings.
- **Quantity** — the amount of Gold you hold, displayed in ounces (**oz**).
- **Total return** — your displayed return in dollars and as a percentage.

The price above the chart is separate from the value of your own holdings in the balance card. Prices and balances in the screenshot are examples.

## Buy Gold

1. Open the Gold screen and tap **Buy/Sell**.
2. Select **Buy**.
3. Enter the dollar amount of Gold you want to purchase, or tap **Use maximum** to use the available balance.
4. Check the funding balance at the bottom of the screen. To buy with Cash, use **Cash balance**.
5. Tap **Continue**.
6. On **Review transaction**, check the purchase amount and funding source, **Gold amount** in ounces, **Gold Price**, **Fees**, and **Processing time**.
7. Tap **Buy** to submit the purchase.

Use the price, fee, and processing estimate shown on your review screen when checking the transaction.

The **Buy/Sell** screen also has a **Sell** tab. A separate **Transfer** button is available on the Gold screen.

## Learn About Your Gold

Scroll to **Description** and tap **Read more** to expand it. The app explains that each PAXG token is backed by one troy ounce of physical gold stored in Brink's vaults in London. Tap **Website** to open the Paxos website for more information.

Below the description, you can expand these questions:

- **How is this different from an ETF?**
- **How does gold generate yield?**
- **How do I redeem physical gold?**

For physical gold redemption help, the app directs you to email [hello@axal.com](mailto:hello@axal.com).

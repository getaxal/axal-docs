The Bitcoin tab shows a complete view of your BTC balance and how it is performing. Here you can see your idle BTC, your yield-earning BTC, and your total earnings to date. You can toggle between a BTC and USD display using the **$ USD** button in the top right.

<img src="/Start%20Earning/btc%20balance%20and%20earning.png" alt="BTC balance and earnings" width="40%" />

## Your BTC Accounts

- **BTC Balance** — the portion of your BTC sitting idle and not yet earning yield
- **BTC Yield account** — your BTC actively deployed into a yield strategy, shown with its current APY

## Start Earning

If you have an idle BTC balance, tap **Start earning →** to move it into a yield position. Your funds will begin generating yield immediately once allocated.

## Key Metrics

- **APY** — your current earning rate on deposited BTC, updated in real time
- **Total Earnings** — the cumulative amount you have earned since depositing
- **Earned today** — the amount earned in the current day, updated throughout the day

## Your Holdings

The Holdings bar at the bottom shows how your BTC is distributed across the underlying yield strategies. Tap **Learn more** to see a full breakdown.

This page explains how to move funds out of Axal. You can withdraw directly to a linked bank account or to an external crypto wallet. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required to complete any withdrawal.

## Withdraw to Bank Account

1. Select the account you would like to withdraw from.

<img src="/Withdrawal/Withdraw_Axal.png" alt="Select account to withdraw from" style={{ width: '40%' }} />

2. Enter the amount you would like to withdraw from your idle balance.

<img src="/Withdrawal/Withdraw_Axal-1.png" alt="Enter withdrawal amount" style={{ width: '40%' }} />

<img src="/Withdrawal/Withdraw_Axal-2.png" alt="Enter withdrawal amount confirmation" style={{ width: '40%' }} />

3. Review the transaction details before confirming.

<img src="/Withdrawal/Withdraw_Axal-3.png" alt="Review transaction" style={{ width: '40%' }} />

<img src="/Withdrawal/Withdraw_Axal-4.png" alt="Review transaction details" style={{ width: '40%' }} />

4. Enter your MFA code to confirm the withdrawal.

## Troubleshooting

- Withdrawal not showing? Allow up to 3–5 business days for bank transfers to settle.
- Wrong network? Contact support at hello@getaxal.com with the transaction hash so the team can advise on recovery options.
- Note: Axal sponsors all gas costs for transactions within the Axal app, not external wallet transactions.

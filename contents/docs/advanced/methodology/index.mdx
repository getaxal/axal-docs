Axal evaluates lending protocols on four things before using them:

- **Safety** — audit history, how long the protocol has held meaningful deposits without
  incident, and the reliability of its price data.
- **Yield sustainability** — whether the yield comes from real borrower interest or from
  temporary incentive programs that decay over time.
- **Diversification value** — how correlated a protocol's risk is to what Axal already uses.
- **Operational fit** — whether the protocol supports the automated rebalancing Axal relies on.

Axal continuously compares rates across its approved protocol set and moves balances toward the
best available safe rate — see [Where Axal's yield comes from](/docs/how-it-works/where-yield-comes-from)
for the full explanation, and [Strategy stack](/docs/how-it-works/strategy-stack) for what's
currently live versus planned.

## Security & audits

Every protocol integration is reviewed before it's added. Axal also undergoes its own independent
audits — see [Audits](/docs/trust-safety/audits).

## Transparency

- Vault addresses are publicly viewable — see [Contracts & addresses](/docs/advanced/contracts).
- APY is reported net of fees.

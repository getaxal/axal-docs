## Currently live

Axal generates USD yield by lending USDC across vetted protocols on Base:

- **Protocols:** Morpho, Euler
- **Network:** Base
- **Strategy:** USDC lending across supported pools on Morpho and Euler, continuously rebalanced
  toward the best safe rate.

## On the roadmap

The following networks and protocols are planned for a future phase and are **not live today**:
ETH Mainnet, Polygon, Arbitrum, Stellar, BNB, Solana, Hyperliquid, Aptos, plus Maple. This section
will move to "Currently live" once shipped — check back for updates rather than treating this list
as available now.

## How protocols are chosen

- **No principal-asset-risk lending only.** Axal only uses lending markets where your deposit
  itself isn't the thing at risk of liquidation — you're the lender, not the borrower.
- Protocols are added only after meeting Axal's safety bar for audit history and track record —
  see [Where yield comes from](/docs/how-it-works/where-yield-comes-from) for how that
  evaluation works.

## Security & transparency

- **Non-custodial:** your funds sit in your own Privy smart wallet — see
  [How your wallet works](/docs/trust-safety/how-your-wallet-works)
- **Automated, not manual:** gas and chain complexity are handled for you; no crypto experience
  required
- **Monitored continuously:** positions are checked on an ongoing basis, not just at deposit time
- **Transparent:** vault addresses are publicly viewable — see
  [Contracts & addresses](/docs/advanced/contracts)

## Email

support@axal.com — for account issues, transaction problems, or anything not covered in these
docs.

## WhatsApp

Message or call anytime — see the **Reach us on WhatsApp** option on this page or in the app for
the current link.

## Before you reach out

For deposit or withdrawal issues, having your transaction hash ready speeds things up
significantly — see [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting) for how to find it.

Axal supports several ways to add a second layer of security to your account, required for
sensitive actions like withdrawals and exporting your wallet.

## Your options

- **Face ID / biometrics** — the fastest option on a phone that supports it. Enable it from
  **Profile → Biometrics**.
- **Passkey** — a device-based credential that works across your devices without typing a code.
  Enable it from **Profile → Multi-factor authentication → Passkey**.
- **Authenticator app** — a standard time-based one-time-passcode (TOTP) app such as Google
  Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password. Enable it from **Profile → Multi-factor authentication →
  Authenticator app**.

Any one of these satisfies Axal's MFA requirement — you don't need to set up more than one, though
you can.

## Why this matters

Withdrawals and wallet exports give complete access to your funds, so Axal requires this extra
step before completing either. An authenticator-style code is time-limited and generated on your
device, so it can't be intercepted the way a text message can.

## Setting up an authenticator app

1. Install an authenticator app of your choice (Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password all
   work) from your phone's app store.
2. In Axal, go to **Profile → Security → Multi-factor authentication** and select **Authenticator
   app**.
3. Scan the QR code shown on screen with your authenticator app.
4. Enter the 6-digit code the app generates to confirm setup.

## Using MFA for a withdrawal or export

When withdrawing or exporting your wallet, you'll be prompted for your current MFA code from
whichever method you enabled (Face ID, passkey, or authenticator app). Enter it to continue.

**Keep your device secure**, don't share your codes with anyone, and if you lose access to your
authenticator device, contact support@axal.com to regain access.

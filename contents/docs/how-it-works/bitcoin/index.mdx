The Bitcoin tab shows your BTC balance and how it's performing — the current price and price
chart, your idle balance, your earning balance with its current APY, and your quantity and total
return.

<img src="/BitcoinPage.PNG" alt="BTC balance and earnings" width="40%" />

## Price chart

At the top, you'll see Bitcoin's current price and how much it's moved over the selected period.
You can view five time periods: **1D**, **7D**, **30D**, **90D**, and **All time**.

## Your balance

- **Idle balance** — the portion of your BTC sitting idle and not yet earning yield
- **Earn balance** — your BTC actively deployed into a yield strategy, shown next to its current APY
- **Quantity** — the raw amount of BTC you hold
- **Total return** — your combined gain from yield earned and any change in Bitcoin's price

## Earn / Pause

Use the **Earn/Pause** button to control your idle balance:

- **Earn** — puts your idle balance to work in a yield strategy
- **Pause** — stops earning and moves your earning balance back to idle

## Buy, sell, and transfer

- **Buy/Sell** — trade in or out of Bitcoin from your Cash balance
- **Transfer** — send or receive BTC to or from an external wallet — see
  [Depositing crypto](/docs/getting-started/depositing-crypto) and
  [How to withdraw](/docs/withdrawing/withdrawal)

## Bringing Bitcoin into Axal

You can deposit BTC you already hold from an external wallet or exchange — see
[Depositing crypto](/docs/getting-started/depositing-crypto). Once it's in Axal, your BTC balance
becomes eligible to earn yield the same way your USD balance does; see
[Where Axal's yield comes from](/docs/how-it-works/where-yield-comes-from) for how that yield is
generated, and [Fees](/docs/how-it-works/fee-structure) for what Axal takes.

Open the **Home** tab using the house icon to see your portfolio at a glance. **Total balance** shows the combined value of your assets, with an earnings summary beneath it.

<img src="/Start%20Earning/portfolio-overview.png" alt="Home screen showing total portfolio balance, the 1D chart, and asset balances" width="40%" />

## Reading the Chart

The green line chart shows your portfolio over time. Choose **1D**, **1W**, **1M**, **90D**, or **ALL** below the chart to change the period.

The earnings summary beneath Total balance changes with the selected period. For example, **1D** shows **Earned today**, while **90D** shows **Earned past 3 months**.

## View Your Assets

Scroll down to **Assets** to see **Cash**, **Bitcoin**, and **Gold**. Each row shows the asset name and the value for the display option you have selected. Where available, the row also shows an APY or the quantity held, such as BTC for Bitcoin and ounces for Gold.

Tap an asset to open its detailed page:

- [Cash](/docs/earning/usd) — balances and Cash earnings.
- [Bitcoin](/docs/earning/bitcoin) — Bitcoin price, holdings, and earnings.
- [Gold](/docs/earning/gold) — Gold price and holdings.

## Change What the Asset List Shows

1. Tap the label and arrows on the right side of the **Assets** heading, such as **Balance** or **Today**.
2. In **Show as**, select the metric you want to view.
3. The asset list updates to show that metric. Reopen the menu to switch views; a checkmark identifies the selected option.

| Show as option | What it shows for each asset |
| --- | --- |
| **Total balance** | The total value of your holdings. |
| **Earning balance** | The balance allocated to earning yield. |
| **Total percent change** | The total change expressed as a percentage. |
| **Today's earnings** | The amount earned today. |
| **Total return (earnings +price)** | The combined return from earnings and price changes. |

<img src="/Start%20Earning/portfolio-display-options.png" alt="Show as menu with total balance, earning balance, total percent change, today's earnings, and total return options" width="40%" />

Changing this display option changes the metric shown beside each asset; it does not move your funds.

## Start Earning and Manage Funds

If a **Start earning** banner appears beneath Cash or Bitcoin, tap it to open that asset's earning flow. Open the asset page to review its idle and earning balances.

Use **Add funds** or **Withdraw** at the bottom of the Home screen to access those flows. A pending bank deposit may also appear in a status banner at the top.

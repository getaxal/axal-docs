The Overview tab shows your combined portfolio at a glance — your total balance and how much
you're earning over time.

## Reading the chart

The solid line is your **total portfolio balance** — principal plus yield earned so far. The bars
show what you earned each day. As you earn more, both trend upward.

You can view five time periods: **7D**, **30D**, **90D**, **1Y**, and **5Y**.

## Assets

Below the chart, your assets are broken out by account:

- **USD Balance** — idle USD not currently earning yield
- **USD Yield account** — USD actively earning yield, with its current APY
- **BTC Balance** — idle BTC not currently earning yield
- **BTC Yield account** — BTC actively earning yield, with its current APY

Tap into the **USD** or **Bitcoin** tabs for a full breakdown of each.

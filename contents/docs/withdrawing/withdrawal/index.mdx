You can withdraw to a linked bank account or to an external crypto wallet. Multi-factor
authentication (MFA) is required to complete any withdrawal.

Only your **idle balance** can be withdrawn directly. If your funds are in a yield position, move
them to idle first (see the USD or Bitcoin page for that asset), then withdraw.

## Troubleshooting

- **Withdrawal not showing?** Allow up to 3–5 business days for bank transfers to settle.
- **Wrong network?** Contact support@axal.com with the transaction hash so the team can advise on
  recovery options.
- Axal covers gas costs for transactions made within the Axal app. Withdrawals to an external
  wallet use that wallet's own network fees on arrival.

This page explains how users move money into Axal directly from a bank account or card.

## Bank Transfer (ACH)

1. Click on Bank Transfer from the Bank Account deposit page.

<img src="/Depositing%20with%20Card/Bank/bankoptions.jpg" alt="Bank Transfer deposit page" width="320" />

2. Verify your identity (note: Axal does not store identification data)

<img src="/Depositing%20with%20Card/Bank/10.jpg" alt="Identity verification" width="320" />

3. Connect your bank account via Plaid.

<img src="/Depositing%20with%20Card/Bank/11.jpg" alt="Connect bank via Plaid" width="320" />

4. Enter and confirm deposit amount.

<img src="/Depositing%20with%20Card/Bank/12.jpg" alt="Enter and confirm deposit amount" width="320" />

## Apple Pay

1. On the deposit panel under Bank Accounts, select Apple Pay.

<img src="/Depositing%20with%20Card/Bank/bankoptions.jpg" alt="Bank Transfer deposit page" width="320" />

2. Enter the amount you would like to deposit.

<img src="/Depositing%20with%20Card/Bank/applepayamt.jpg" alt="Enter deposit amount" width="320" />

3. Enter your phone number and the verification code.

<img src="/Depositing%20with%20Card/Bank/Screenshot_2025-09-05_at_3.40.53_PM.png" alt="Enter mobile number" width="320" />

<img src="/Depositing%20with%20Card/Bank/Screenshot_2025-09-05_at_3.41.07_PM.png" alt="Verify mobile number" width="320" />

4. If you're on mobile, you'll be prompted to transact the amount of money you input through Apple Pay on Coinbase. There will be no transaction fees since it is USDC on Base.

<img src="/Depositing%20with%20Card/Bank/Screenshot_2025-09-05_at_3.41.26_PM.png" alt="Apple Pay transaction" width="320" />

5. If you're on a laptop, an Apple Pay code will open for you to scan with a phone. That will open the same prompt on your phone.

<img src="/Depositing%20with%20Card/Bank/Screenshot_2025-09-05_at_3.42.58_PM.png" alt="Apple Pay QR code" width="320" />

## Credit/Debit Card

1. On the deposit panel under Bank Accounts, select Credit/Debit Card.

<img src="/Depositing%20with%20Card/Bank/bankoptions.jpg" alt="Select Credit/Debit Card" width="320" />

2. Enter the amount you would like to deposit.

<img src="/Depositing%20with%20Card/Bank/pay.jpg" alt="Enter deposit amount" width="320" />

3. You will be taken to Coinbase where you will fill in your card details.

<img src="/Depositing%20with%20Card/Bank/Screenshot_2025-09-05_at_3.47.27_PM.png" alt="Payment method selection" width="320" />

4. Proceed to buy USDC with your credit card.

<img src="/Depositing%20with%20Card/Bank/Screenshot_2025-09-05_at_3.47.44_PM.png" alt="Buy USDC with card" width="320" />

## Wire Transfer, SEPA, PIX, or SPEI

1. On the deposit panel under Bank Accounts, select Wire Transfer

<img src="/Depositing%20with%20Card/Bank/bankoptions.jpg" alt="Select Wire Transfer" width="320" />

2. Initiate a wire transfer from your bank account.

3. Copy and paste the displayed Routing and Account Number information for funds to be deposited into your Axal account.

<img src="/Depositing%20with%20Card/Bank/wire.jpg" alt="Routing and Account Number information" width="320" />

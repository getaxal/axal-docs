## Bank transfer (ACH)

1. From **Add funds**, select **Bank Transfer**.
2. Verify your identity. This step is handled by Axal's banking partners (not stored by Axal
   itself) and is required to move money between the traditional banking system and your account —
   see [Partners](/docs/trust-safety/partners) for who's involved.
3. Connect your bank account.
4. Enter and confirm the deposit amount.

## Apple Pay

1. From **Add funds**, select **Apple Pay**.
2. Enter the amount you'd like to deposit.
3. Enter your phone number and the verification code.

4. On mobile, you'll be prompted to complete the payment through Apple Pay. Your deposit lands as
   USDC on Base, with no network transaction fee.
5. On a laptop, an Apple Pay code opens for you to scan with your phone, which continues the same
   flow there.

## Wire transfer, SEPA, PIX, or SPEI

1. From **Add funds**, select **Wire Transfer**.
2. Initiate a wire transfer from your bank account.
3. Copy the displayed routing and account number into your bank's transfer form.

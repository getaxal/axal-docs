## Create your account

1. Sign up or log in.
2. Enter the one-time passcode sent to your email.
3. Your self-custodial wallet is created automatically — no seed phrase to write down yet, and
   nothing to install.

## Make your first deposit

Add at least $2 using any of these methods:

- **Bank or card** — see [Adding money by bank or card](/docs/getting-started/depositing-with-card-bank)
- **From an app you already use** (CashApp, Coinbase, Robinhood) — see [Adding money from another app](/docs/getting-started/depositing-from-app)
- **Crypto transfer** — see [Depositing crypto](/docs/getting-started/depositing-crypto)

Once your deposit lands, it starts as an idle balance. Tap **Start earning** to move it into a
yield position — see [Where Axal's yield comes from](/docs/how-it-works/where-yield-comes-from)
for how that yield is generated.

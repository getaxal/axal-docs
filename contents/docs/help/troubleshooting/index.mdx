## Deposit isn't showing up

Check the transaction hash by opening the desktop/browser app and clicking **View on Explorer**.
If the transaction is confirmed on-chain but still not reflected in Axal after a few minutes,
contact support@axal.com with the transaction hash.

## Sent on the wrong network

Contact support@axal.com with the transaction hash as soon as possible. Recovery isn't always
possible, but the team can advise on options for your specific case.

## Withdrawal is taking a while

Bank withdrawals typically take 3–5 business days to settle. Crypto withdrawals confirm on-chain
within minutes of you approving them — if it's been longer than that, check the transaction hash
on the network's block explorer before contacting support.

## Can't complete MFA / lost my authenticator device

Contact support@axal.com to regain access to your account. See
[Face ID, passkeys & 2FA](/docs/trust-safety/authenticator) for the options available going
forward once you're back in.

## Balance looks wrong after a rebalance

Axal automatically moves your yield-earning balance between protocols to keep it earning the best
safe rate — this can show up as a small, expected shift in your position. See
[Where Axal's yield comes from](/docs/how-it-works/where-yield-comes-from). If something looks
wrong beyond that, contact support@axal.com.

## App won't load or keeps signing me out

Try refreshing or reopening the app. If it persists, contact support@axal.com with your device
type and, if possible, a screenshot.

## Still stuck?

Reach us at support@axal.com, or see [Contact & support](/docs/help/contact) for other ways to get
in touch.

This page explains how users move money into Axal directly from a bank account or card.

## Bank Transfer (ACH)

1. From Add funds, Click on Bank Transfer.
2. Verify your identity (note: Axal does not store identification data)
3. Connect your bank account via Link.
4. Enter and confirm deposit amount.

## Apple Pay

1. From Add funds, select Apple Pay.
2. Enter the amount you would like to deposit.
3. Enter your phone number and the verification code.

4. If you're on mobile, you'll be prompted to transact the amount of money you input through Apple Pay on Coinbase. There will be no transaction fees since it is USDC on Base.

5. If you're on a laptop, an Apple Pay code will open for you to scan with a phone. That will open the same prompt on your phone.

## Wire Transfer, SEPA, PIX, or SPEI

1. From Add funds, select Wire Transfer
2. Initiate a wire transfer from your bank account.
3. Copy and paste the displayed Routing and Account Number information for funds to be deposited into your Axal account.

This walks you through moving cash or crypto you already hold in CashApp, Coinbase, or Robinhood
into Axal.

## Send cash

Transfers dollars directly from your CashApp, Coinbase, or Robinhood balance. Under the hood, this
still moves through a payment partner rail (the same as a card or bank deposit) — it isn't a
crypto transfer.

1. From **Add funds**, select **From an app** — choose CashApp, Coinbase, or Robinhood.
2. Select your asset — choose **Cash**.
3. Enter the amount you'd like to transfer.
4. Review and confirm.

## Send crypto

Transfers an existing crypto asset from CashApp, Coinbase, or Robinhood into Axal.

1. From **Add funds**, select **From an app** — choose CashApp, Coinbase, or Robinhood.
2. Select your asset — choose any asset other than Cash.
3. Select your blockchain network. **The network must match on both sides** — sending on a
   network Axal doesn't support, or a different network than the one you selected, can delay or
   strand your transfer.
4. Copy the wallet address or scan the QR code to send from your external app.

## Troubleshooting

- **Transfer not showing?** Check the transaction hash by opening the desktop/browser app and
  clicking **View on Explorer**.
- **Sent on the wrong network?** Contact support@axal.com with the transaction hash so the team
  can advise on recovery options.
- Axal covers gas costs for transactions made within the Axal app. Transfers you send from an
  external wallet or app use that platform's own fees.

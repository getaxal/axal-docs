Invite a friend to Axal and you can both receive a **+1.00% APY boost on Cash for 30 days**. Your friend must be a new user who signs up with your referral code, and both of you must verify your identities to qualify.

<img src="/Referral%20Program/boost-details.png" alt="Referral boost details showing friends referred, total earned, the Cash APY boost rules, and code-sharing controls" width="40%" />

## How It Works

1. **Share your referral code** with a friend who is new to Axal.
2. **Your friend signs up with your code and verifies their identity.** You must also have verified your identity.
3. **You both receive a +1.00% APY Cash boost for 30 days** once you are both verified.

Every new qualifying referral adds another **30 days** to your boost. Referrals can be stacked up to **4 times**. Stacking extends the boost's duration; the boost remains +1.00% APY.

## Share Your Code

1. Open the **Rewards** tab using the gift icon in the Axal app.
2. Find the **Referral program** card.
3. Tap **Copy** beside your referral code to copy it, or tap **Share** to open your phone's sharing options.
4. Send the invitation to your friend so they can sign up with your code.

You can also copy your code or tap **Share code** from the **Boost details** screen.

## Track Your Referrals and Earnings

From **Rewards**, tap **Boost details** in the **Referral program** card. This screen shows:

- **Friends referred:** the number of friends you have referred.
- **Total earned:** your referral boost earnings.
- **How it works:** the verification requirements and rules for stacking referrals.

The Referral program card also shows when a referred friend still needs to verify their identity. A friend appearing in your referral count does not mean their boost has started; both of you must be verified.

### View Payout History

1. Open **Rewards** → **Boost details**.
2. Tap **See payout history**.
3. Review the displayed date range and **Total earned this period**. If there have been no boost payouts for that period, the screen shows **No boost payouts in this period yet**.

## Terms

- Referrals apply to new users who sign up with your code.
- Both you and your friend must verify your identities to qualify for the boost.
- Each qualifying referral provides 30 days of boost time, with stacking available up to 4 times.
- Tap **Terms apply** in **Boost details** to review the program terms.

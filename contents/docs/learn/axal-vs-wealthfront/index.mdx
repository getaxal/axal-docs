Wealthfront Cash is an FDIC-insured cash account from a well-known investing app. Axal pays roughly twice the rate, but it isn't FDIC insured. The right choice depends on whether protection or yield matters more for a given pile of money.

| | Wealthfront Cash | Axal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rate | 3.55% APY base, as of Oct 1, 2026, plus temporary boosts for new clients | About 7.6% APY, variable, as of October 2026 |
| Deposit insurance | FDIC insurance eligibility up to $8 million, through program banks | Not FDIC insured |
| Account fees | None | None |
| Withdrawals | Instant to eligible banks; otherwise about one business day | Anytime, no penalties; 1–3 business days to your bank |
| Where interest comes from | Deposits swept to partner banks | Overcollateralized lending markets |
| Also offers | Investing and other Wealthfront products | Bitcoin and gold in the same app |

Wealthfront details are from the [Wealthfront Cash page](https://www.wealthfront.com/cash), checked October 2026. Check their site for current rates and terms.

## When Wealthfront Cash is the better choice

- You want FDIC insurance on large balances
- You need instant withdrawals to your bank
- You already invest with Wealthfront

## When Axal may fit

- You want a meaningfully higher rate on savings beyond your emergency fund
- You're comfortable with a non-bank product and its risks
- You want cash, Bitcoin and gold in one app

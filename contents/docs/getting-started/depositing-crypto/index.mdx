This guide walks you through transferring USDC into Axal. Your self-custodial wallet is created
automatically when you sign up — you fund it by scanning the wallet QR code or copying its address
from your external wallet or exchange.

## What you need

- An Axal account
- USDC on one of Axal's supported networks. **Base is the default and recommended network** — it
  has the lowest fees and is what most in-app flows are optimized for. Ethereum, Arbitrum, Monad,
  and Polygon are also supported.
- A wallet or exchange that can send USDC on one of the above networks.

## Secure your wallet first

We strongly recommend securing your wallet before depositing meaningful amounts:

1. Go to **Profile**.
2. Enable **Biometrics** (Face ID), or
3. Enable **Multi-factor authentication** — passkey or authenticator app.

If you're sending a large amount for the first time, set this up before you send — see
[Face ID, passkeys & 2FA](/docs/trust-safety/authenticator).

## Deposit with a crypto wallet

1. From **Add funds**, choose **Crypto Transfer**.
2. Select your network — Base if you're not sure.
3. Scan the QR code, or copy the wallet address into your external wallet's withdrawal page.

## Troubleshooting

- **Deposit not showing?** Check the transaction hash by opening the desktop/browser app and
  clicking **View on Explorer**. Your transfer should appear under Token Holdings once confirmed.

![Deposit not showing troubleshooting](</Depositting%20Crypto(Images)/Screen%20Shot%202025-09-09%20at%201.33.12%20AM.png>)

- **Sent on the wrong network?** Contact support@axal.com with the transaction hash so the team
  can advise on recovery options.
- Axal covers gas costs for transactions made within the Axal app. Transfers sent from an external
  wallet use that wallet's own network fees.

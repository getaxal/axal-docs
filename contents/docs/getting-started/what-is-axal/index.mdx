Axal is an automated savings app. You deposit dollars, Bitcoin, or gold, and Axal puts that
balance to work in vetted yield strategies instead of letting it sit idle — so you earn more than
a typical bank account or brokerage cash balance, without picking protocols or managing positions
yourself.

You stay in control the whole time. Your funds sit in your own self-custodial wallet — Axal never
holds your money, and you can export your wallet or withdraw at any time. Axal's job is to route
your balance to the best available yield within a fixed set of rules, watch it continuously, and
pull out if something looks wrong.

## What you can do in Axal

- **Add money** — from a bank account, debit card, or an existing crypto wallet
- **Earn yield** — on USD, Bitcoin, and (coming soon) gold and tokenized stocks
- **Withdraw anytime** — to your bank account or to any external crypto wallet
- **Track performance** — see your balance, daily earnings, and APY in one place

## Fee overview

Axal takes a **15% fee on the yield you earn** — not on your deposit, and not as a subscription.
There are no upfront fees and no withdrawal fees. If you don't earn anything, Axal doesn't charge
anything.

## A worked example

Say your balance earns **$100** in yield over some period:

- Axal takes **$15** (15% of the $100 earned)
- You keep **$85**

Your principal is never touched by this fee — it only applies to the yield generated on top of it,
and only after it's earned.

Axal reserves the right to update its fee structure going forward; any change would apply to
future yield, not retroactively.

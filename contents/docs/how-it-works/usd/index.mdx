The Cash tab shows your dollar balance and how it's performing — your idle balance, your earning
balance with its current APY, and what you've earned today and over the life of the account.

<img src="/CashPage.PNG" alt="USD balance and earnings" width="40%" />

## Your balance

- **Idle balance** — the portion of your USD sitting idle and not yet earning yield
- **Earn balance** — your USD actively deployed into a yield strategy, shown next to its current APY

## Earn / Pause

Use the **Earn/Pause** button to control your idle balance:

- **Earn** — puts your idle balance to work in a yield strategy
- **Pause** — stops earning and moves your earning balance back to idle

## Today's and lifetime earnings

Below your balance, you'll see:

- **Today's earnings** — what you've earned so far today
- **Lifetime earnings** — the cumulative amount you've earned since depositing

## Earnings details

Tap the earnings widget (the weekly chart showing what you earned this week) to see a detailed
breakdown, including:

- Lifetime earnings, earning rate, and projected earnings per year
- **Investments** — where your strategy currently distributes your balance, broken out by pool
  (for example Morpho Blue markets), each shown with its own APY

<img src="/CashPage_earning.PNG" alt="Earnings details breakdown" width="40%" />

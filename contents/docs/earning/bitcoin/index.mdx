Open **Bitcoin** under **Assets** on the home screen to view its price, your balance, and your earnings.

<img src="/Start%20Earning/bitcoin-overview.png" alt="Bitcoin screen showing the price chart, Start earning banner, Idle balance, and Earn balance" width="40%" />

## Price and Balance

The chart at the top shows Bitcoin's price and its change over the selected period. Choose **1D**, **7D**, **30D**, **90D**, or **All time** to change the period.

Below the chart, the balance card shows:

- **Balance** — the dollar value of your Bitcoin holdings.
- **Idle balance** — Bitcoin that is not currently earning yield.
- **Earn balance** — Bitcoin allocated to earning yield, alongside its earning rate.
- **Quantity** — the amount of Bitcoin you hold, shown in BTC.
- **Total return** — your displayed return in dollars and as a percentage.

The Bitcoin price at the top of the screen is separate from the value of your own holdings in the balance card. Rates and values in the screenshot are examples; check the app for current values.

## Start Earning

1. Tap **Start earning** in the idle-balance banner, or tap **Earn/Pause** in the balance card and select **Earn**.
2. Enter the dollar amount of Bitcoin you want to move into your Earn balance. Tap **Use maximum** to use the available Bitcoin balance shown on the screen.
3. Tap **Continue**.
4. On **Review transaction**, check the amount moving from **Idle Balance** to **Earn Balance**, along with the APY, fees, and processing time.
5. **Swipe up to earn** to confirm.
6. When **Earnings initiated!** appears, tap anywhere to continue. The confirmation says your amount will start earning shortly.

Return to the Bitcoin screen to see your updated Idle balance and Earn balance. The **Earn/Pause** control also provides a **Pause** option.

## Track Your Earnings

Scroll to the **Earnings** card to see the amount earned **This week** and a daily bar chart. Tap **All time** on this card to open the detailed **Earnings** screen.

Under **Axal Prime**, you can review:

- **Lifetime earnings** — your total earnings for the strategy.
- **Earning rate** — the current rate.
- **Asset** — BTC.
- **Earnings balance** — the balance allocated to earning, displayed in dollars.
- **Projected earnings** — the displayed yearly estimate, which can change with your balance and earning rate.

Scroll down to the **Earnings** list to see daily earnings by date. **Earn** and **Pause** controls are also available at the bottom of this screen.

## View Your Investments

On the detailed Earnings screen, **Investments** shows where the strategy distributes your assets. The allocation bar and investment list show the underlying markets, their allocation percentages, and rates. Each investment has a details arrow.

## Learn About Bitcoin

Scroll down on the Bitcoin screen to **Description** and tap **Read more** to expand it. Below the description, you can open answers to these questions:

- **Who controls my Bitcoin?**
- **How does Axal get me BTC?**
- **How does BTC yield work?**

Use **Buy/Sell** or **Transfer** at the bottom of the Bitcoin screen to access those flows.

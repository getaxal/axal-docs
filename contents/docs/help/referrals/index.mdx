Refer friends to Axal and both of you earn. For every friend you invite who makes a deposit, your friend receives $5 in BTC and you earn $5 in BTC — automatically.

This is a one-time cash bonus for the referral, not a boost to your ongoing yield rate — your APY
on deposited funds works the same way whether or not you've referred anyone. See
[Where Axal's yield comes from](/docs/how-it-works/where-yield-comes-from) for how your actual
yield is generated.

## How It Works

1. **Share your invite link or referral code** with a friend.
2. **Your friend gets $5 in BTC** when they make their first deposit.
3. **You earn $5 in BTC** for every successful referral.

<img src="/Referral%20Program/referral.jpg" alt="Referral program screen" width="40%" />

## Getting Started

1. Go to the **Rewards** tab in the Axal app.
2. Copy your unique referral link using the **Copy Link** button.
3. Share it with friends — you can track your Friends Referred and Rewards Earned directly on the referrals screen.

## Terms

- Referral reward is triggered when the invited friend makes their first deposit.
- Both the referrer and referee must maintain active accounts.
- Standard Axal terms and conditions apply.

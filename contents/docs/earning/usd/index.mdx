The USD tab shows a complete view of your dollar balance and how it is performing. Here you can see your idle USD, your yield-earning USD, and your total earnings to date.

<img src="/Start%20Earning/USD%20earning.png" alt="USD balance and earnings" style={{ width: '40%' }} />

## Your USD Accounts

- **USD Balance** — the portion of your USD sitting idle and not yet earning yield
- **USD Yield account** — your USD actively deployed into a yield strategy, shown with its current APY

## Start Earning

If you have an idle USD balance, tap **Start earning →** to move it into a yield position. Your funds will begin generating yield immediately once allocated.

## Key Metrics

- **APY** — your current earning rate on deposited USD, updated in real time
- **Total Earnings** — the cumulative dollar amount you have earned since depositing
- **Earned today** — the amount earned in the current day, updated throughout the day

## Your Holdings

The Holdings bar at the bottom shows how your USD is distributed across the underlying yield strategies. Tap **Learn more** to see a full breakdown.

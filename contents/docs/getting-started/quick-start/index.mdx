## What is Axal?

Axal is building the future of wealth — leveraging stablecoins, blockchains, and real assets to automate, secure, and grow your money. Instead of leaving your money idle in a traditional bank, you put it to work in Axal. Behind the scenes, Axal automatically deploys your funds across safe, reliable strategies in dollars, Bitcoin, gold, and a growing universe of assets.

Think of it as your personal wealth engine, constantly optimizing across asset classes so you don't have to.

You always stay in control. Every Axal wallet comes with a private key that only you hold, and every transaction is reviewed by an intelligent security layer before it executes, protecting your wealth without adding complexity. No networks to manage. No fees to think about. No compromises on control.

As Axal expands into new assets, your portfolio expands with it. One elegant, unified platform to hold, grow, and diversify, with integrated yield on every asset for the confidence that your money is always working harder than it would anywhere else.

Axal. Your wealth, optimized.

## Getting Started

Follow these steps to start earning with Axal:

1. Create/login to your account
2. Enter the one-time passcode sent to your email inbox.
3. Explore the dashboard.
4. Make your first deposit (minimum of $2) through bank, crypto transfer, or existing applications like Coinbase or Robinhood.

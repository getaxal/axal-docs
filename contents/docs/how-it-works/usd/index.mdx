The USD tab shows your dollar balance and how it's performing — your idle USD, your yield-earning
USD, and your total earnings to date.

<img src="/Start%20Earning/USD%20earning.png" alt="USD balance and earnings" width="40%" />

## Your USD accounts

- **USD Balance** — the portion of your USD sitting idle and not yet earning yield
- **USD Yield account** — your USD actively deployed into a yield strategy, with its current APY

## Start earning

If you have an idle USD balance, tap **Start earning →** to move it into a yield position. It
begins earning as soon as it's allocated.

## Key metrics

- **APY** — your current earning rate on deposited USD, updated in real time
- **Total Earnings** — the cumulative dollar amount you've earned since depositing
- **Earned today** — the amount earned so far today

## Your holdings

The Holdings bar at the bottom shows how your USD is distributed across the underlying yield
strategies. Tap **Learn more** for a full breakdown.

## How this yield is generated

Your USD yield comes from lending USDC into established DeFi protocols, where borrowers pay
interest on the funds they borrow. Axal continuously monitors rates and moves your balance toward
the best available safe rate — see [Where Axal's yield comes from](/docs/how-it-works/where-yield-comes-from)
for the full explanation, and [Risks](/docs/trust-safety/risks) for what could go wrong.

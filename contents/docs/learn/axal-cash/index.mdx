## How your cash earns 7.6%

Banks lend out your deposits and keep most of the interest. Axal lends your dollars directly to borrowers through established lending markets, and every borrower has to put up more collateral than they borrow. The interest they pay goes to you, and Axal takes no cut. [How Axal Works](/docs/learn/how-axal-works) explains it in more detail.

## What to know before you deposit

Axal isn't a bank, and your balance isn't FDIC insured. The rate changes with lending demand. Lending markets carry software, dollar-peg and market risks that bank accounts don't. Read [Is Axal Safe?](/docs/learn/is-axal-safe)

## Common questions

**Can I withdraw anytime?** Yes, with no penalties. Bank withdrawals take 1–3 business days.

**Is there a minimum?** No. Start with any amount.

**Is there a holding period on deposits?** No. Deposits are processed through Stripe, and there's no waiting period before you can withdraw.

**Do I need to know crypto?** No. You deposit and withdraw dollars.

**How do I start?** Download Axal on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/axal-high-yield-savings/id6752484843) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.axal.android), sign up with email, and deposit.

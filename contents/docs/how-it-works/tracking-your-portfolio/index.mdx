The Overview tab shows your combined portfolio at a glance — your total balance and how much
you're earning over time.

## Reading the chart

The solid line is your **total portfolio balance** — principal plus yield earned so far. As you earn more, both trend upward.

You can view five time periods: **1D** **1W**, **1M**, **90D**, and **All time**.

## Assets

Below the chart, your assets are listed one row per asset (Cash, Bitcoin, Gold), each showing its
balance, how much of that balance is earning versus idle, and its total return.

The **Balance** column header has a dropdown — tap it to change which metric is shown for every
asset in the list:

- **Total balance** — your full balance for that asset, idle plus earning
- **Earning balance** — just the portion actively deployed and earning yield
- **Total % change** — your return on that asset as a percentage
- **Today's earnings** — what that asset earned so far today
- **Total return (earnings + price)** — your combined gain from yield earned and any price
  movement of the asset itself (relevant for Bitcoin and Gold, not for Cash)

Tap into an individual asset for a full breakdown, including its idle and yield-earning balances
and current APY — see [USD](/docs/how-it-works/usd) or [Bitcoin](/docs/how-it-works/bitcoin).

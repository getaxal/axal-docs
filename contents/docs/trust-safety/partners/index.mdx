Axal works with a small set of partners to handle wallets, payments, and security — each doing
one job well rather than Axal building everything in-house.

### Privy — wallet infrastructure

Privy provides the self-custodial wallet technology behind every Axal account. It's what lets you
sign in with email or a passkey and get a working crypto wallet without installing a browser
extension or managing a seed phrase yourself. Privy does not have custody of your funds — see
[How your wallet works](/docs/trust-safety/how-your-wallet-works).

### Plaid, Astra, Bridge, Coinbase — payment rails

These partners handle moving money between the traditional banking system and Axal — bank
verification, ACH transfers, and card payments. They're also the ones responsible for the identity
verification step during bank or card deposits, run under standard financial compliance
requirements.

### Sherlock — security auditor & bug bounty host

Sherlock is a web3 security firm that audits Axal's signer and infrastructure, and hosts Axal's
live bug bounty program. Sherlock is **not an insurance provider** — it does not cover or reimburse
losses. See [Audits](/docs/trust-safety/audits) for the audit report and bounty details.

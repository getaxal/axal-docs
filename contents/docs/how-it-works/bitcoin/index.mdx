The Bitcoin tab shows your BTC balance and how it's performing — your idle BTC, your yield-earning
BTC, and your total earnings to date. Toggle between BTC and USD display with the **$ USD** button
in the top right.

<img src="/Start%20Earning/btc%20balance%20and%20earning.png" alt="BTC balance and earnings" width="40%" />

## Your BTC accounts

- **BTC Balance** — the portion of your BTC sitting idle and not yet earning yield
- **BTC Yield account** — your BTC actively deployed into a yield strategy, with its current APY

## Start earning

If you have an idle BTC balance, tap **Start earning →** to move it into a yield position. It
begins earning as soon as it's allocated.

## Key metrics

- **APY** — your current earning rate on deposited BTC, updated in real time
- **Total Earnings** — the cumulative amount you've earned since depositing
- **Earned today** — the amount earned so far today

## Your holdings

The Holdings bar at the bottom shows how your BTC is distributed across the underlying yield
strategies. Tap **Learn more** for a full breakdown.

## Bringing Bitcoin into Axal

You can deposit BTC you already hold from an external wallet or exchange — see
[Depositing crypto](/docs/getting-started/depositing-crypto). Once it's in Axal, your BTC balance
becomes eligible to earn yield the same way your USD balance does; see
[Where Axal's yield comes from](/docs/how-it-works/where-yield-comes-from) for how that yield is
generated, and [Fees](/docs/how-it-works/fee-structure) for what Axal takes.

Axal lets you hold cash, Bitcoin and gold in one account. Your cash earns a variable 7.6% APY, Bitcoin can earn yield too, and you can buy real gold without a vault or broker.

| Asset | What you can do | Earns yield? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cash (dollars) | Deposit, earn, withdraw anytime | Yes, about 7.6% APY, variable |
| Bitcoin | Buy, hold, sell, transfer | Yes, variable; see the current rate in the app |
| Gold (Paxos Gold, PAXG) | Buy, hold, sell, transfer | Not yet; gold yield is coming soon |

## Cash

Your dollars are lent through overcollateralized lending markets, and the interest goes to you. There's no minimum and no fee. See [Axal Cash](/docs/learn/axal-cash).

## Bitcoin

Buy Bitcoin at market quotes with no extra spread added by Axal, then put it to work earning yield without selling it. See [Bitcoin](/docs/earning/bitcoin).

## Gold

Gold in Axal is held as Paxos Gold (PAXG). Each token represents one fine troy ounce of a London Good Delivery gold bar stored in Brink's vaults, issued by Paxos Trust Company and regulated by the New York Department of Financial Services. See [Gold](/docs/earning/gold).

## Why one app?

Most people juggle a bank for cash, an exchange for Bitcoin, and a broker or dealer for gold. Axal puts all three in one place, with one balance view and no account minimums.

## What to know

Axal isn't a bank, and balances aren't FDIC insured. Bitcoin and gold prices go up and down, so their value can fall. Read [Is Axal Safe?](/docs/learn/is-axal-safe)

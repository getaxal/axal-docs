Axal is working with [Dinari](https://www.dinari.com/) to bring tokenized US stock exposure into
the app. This is **not yet available** — this page describes what's planned, not a live feature.

## What this will be

Tokenized stocks represent ownership of a real underlying US equity, issued on-chain by a
regulated partner. The goal is to let you hold and (eventually) earn on stock exposure alongside
your USD, Bitcoin, and gold balances, inside the same account.

## What this will not be

- Not a synthetic or derivative product that merely tracks a stock's price without backing.
- Not available for trading or deposits yet — there is no live APY, ticker list, or fee schedule
  to share until this launches.

We'll update this page with real details — supported tickers, custody model, and how yield (if
any) works — once the feature ships.
